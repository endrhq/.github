## 「 01 」「 COMPANY BRIEF 」

endr is a Seattle-based battlefield AI company developing software for bounded human–AI mission coordination and execution when communications are unreliable, resources are limited, and information is uncertain.

## 「 02 」「 FIRST PRODUCT 」

**Mission Assurance Lab (M.A.L.)** helps evaluation and integration engineers answer a specific question: after an agent, model, adapter, or policy change, does a configured workflow still meet its declared task and authority requirements under defined faults?

The intended output is a reproducible comparison and failure-evidence package for a human engineering acceptance decision. M.A.L. is endr’s first product. Resilient edge execution is a later objective, guided by evidence and customer need.

## 「 03 」「 COMMAND PRINCIPLES 」

- **People set the bounds.** An authorized human owns consequential decisions and engineering acceptance.
- **Lost links never expand authority.** Degraded communications preserve explicit limits.
- **Decisions leave evidence.** Attempts, policy decisions, observed effects, and interventions need attributable records.

## 「 04 」「 RESEARCH DIRECTION 」

The first proposed research case is synthetic supply-task scheduling: fictional inventory and maintenance requests feed bounded scheduling decisions. The engineering comparison would hold initial conditions and fault schedules constant across a simple baseline, a reference configuration, and a candidate change. Scenario acceptance and performance thresholds remain open.

The [Mission Assurance Lab repository](https://github.com/endrhq/mission-assurance-lab) is private. Authorized collaborators can find the current draft validator, synthetic fixtures, architecture, and review plan there.

## 「 05 」「 EVIDENCE AND LIMITS 」

The Lab repository currently implements draft-contract consistency checks and a read-only CLI. Mission execution, fault simulation, runtime authority enforcement, operator controls, and replay are not implemented by that tooling. The diagram above is conceptual and contains no live simulation result. [Read the scoped evidence record](https://github.com/endrhq/.github/blob/main/%5Bendr%5D%5Brepository%5D%5Bprofile-status%5D.md).
